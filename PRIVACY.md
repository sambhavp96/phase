# Privacy Policy for Phase

**Effective date:** 4 November 2026

Phase is a period tracker for one person and, if they choose, one partner. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions: **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## The short version

- Phase stores only what you enter: your name, your email, and your period dates and notes.
- Only you and the one partner you connect with can see your cycle data.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
- You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Email address and password | To create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our database provider; we cannot see them. |
| First name | Shown to your partner, and on entries you log. |
| Period start and end dates, and optional notes | To show your history, calculate averages, predict your next period and estimate cycle phases. |
| Which partner you are connected to, and invite codes | To share data between exactly two accounts. |
| Technical data needed to run the service (for example sign-in times and IP addresses in server logs) | Kept by our database provider for security and reliability. |

Phase does **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, health data from other apps, advertising identifiers or usage analytics.

Predictions and phases are calculated from the dates you log. They are estimates, not medical advice, and must not be used as contraception.

## Who can see your data

- **You.**
- **Your partner**, only if you connect with them using an invite code. A connected partner can see and add to the cycle history. The person whose cycle is tracked can disconnect the partner at any time, and they lose access immediately.
- **Our database provider, Supabase**, which stores the data on our behalf and processes it only to run the service. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. It is hosted in **[SUPABASE REGION, e.g. United States (East)]**.

Nobody else can see your data. Database rules enforce this on the server: every couple's data is invisible to every other account.

We will only disclose data if we are legally required to, for example by a valid court order. Where the law allows, we will tell you first. Because Phase keeps only what you enter, there is very little to disclose, and data you have deleted cannot be recovered.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it. You can:

- **Delete a single period** from the log screen.
- **Delete all cycle data** (if it is your cycle) from Menu → Partner and sharing.
- **Delete your account** from Menu → Delete account. This removes your account immediately. If the cycle is yours, your whole history is deleted and your partner is disconnected. If you are the partner, your account is removed and the history stays with the person whose cycle it is.

Deleted data may remain in our provider's encrypted backups for a short period before it is overwritten.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to how it is used. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, email **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## Children

Phase is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
