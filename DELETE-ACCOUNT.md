# Delete your Phase account

This page explains how to delete your **Phase** account and the data linked to it. Phase is a period tracker app for Android and iOS.

## Option 1: in the app (immediate)

1. Open Phase and sign in.
2. Tap the **menu** icon (top right of the home screen).
3. Tap **Delete account**, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email **sambhavpokharel@gmail.com** from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject **Delete my Phase account**. We will confirm the request is yours, delete the account within 7 days, and reply when it is done.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in details (email address and password).
- Your name.
- **If the cycle is yours:** every period you or your partner logged, with any notes, and the connection to your partner. Your partner loses access immediately.
- **If you follow a partner's cycle:** your account and your connection to them. The cycle history belongs to your partner and stays with them; periods you logged remain in their history without your name.
- Any unused invite codes.

## What is kept

Nothing is kept on purpose. Deleted data may remain in our database provider's encrypted backups for a short period until they are overwritten, and is never restored into the app.

If you only want to remove your cycle data and keep your account, use **Menu → Partner and sharing → Delete all cycle data** instead.
